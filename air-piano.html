🎹 Air Piano Instructions

How to Play:

  • Position your hand: Hold your hand in front of the webcam with your index finger pointing.
  • Move horizontally: Move your finger left to right to select different notes (C, D, E, F, G, A, B).
  • Tap to play: Make a quick downward motion with your index finger to play the note.
  • Watch the indicator: A golden circle shows where your finger is detected on screen.

Game Modes:

  • Free Play: Play any notes freely by moving and tapping your finger.
  • Melody Challenge: Reproduce the sequence shown on screen. Match the notes in order!

Tips:

  • Ensure good lighting for better hand tracking
  • Keep your hand clearly visible in the webcam
  • Make distinct downward tapping motions
  • The tap detection works best with quick, deliberate movements
